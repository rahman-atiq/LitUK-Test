Every day, without being toldfive standing rules

  • 🤔 Tap “Wasn’t sure” on every lucky guess It blocks the day’s credit and costs 12% in the model. A guess you don’t declare becomes a question the app thinks you know.
  • 🐛 Leeches need the explicit tap Missed four or more times, and they never clear on the counter. Read the explanation properly, then “I know this now”.
  • 📊 Trust pass probability, not tests passed That, and expected score out of 24. “Tests passed” is a footnote on the dashboard for a reason — it counts retries of the same 24 questions.
  • 🗣 Compare pass probability out loud Separate phones, separate stores, separate ticks on this page. Whoever is behind gets the longer evening.
